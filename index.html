Who We Are

A Community
Built on Love

For over a century, Grace Cathedral has been a cornerstone of faith and fellowship in our city. We believe every person carries an eternal worth — and our mission is to celebrate that dignity through worship, community, and service.

We are an inclusive congregation rooted in the ancient Christian tradition, yet open to the questions and struggles of modern life. Whether you are returning home or finding faith for the first time, you are welcome here.

Youth & Family Pastor

Pastor Paul King

Pastor Paul brings infectious energy and deep theological conviction to his work with the next generation. Under his leadership, the youth ministry has grown to over 300 young people and has become one of the most vibrant communities in the region.

Born in Lagos, Nigeria, Paul came to faith at age 16 and has dedicated his life to helping young people discover their God-given identity and purpose. He holds a degree in Youth Ministry from Wheaton College.
